    2 Format

    What kind of experience is this? Agencies build itineraries from half-days, full days and evenings.

    3 Day program

    Plan the whole experience step by step, so the traveler doesn't have to organize anything. For a full day, combine several activities — for example a market walk, a workshop and lunch.

    4 Prices — your price, in yen

    Enter the price you want to receive. Keep prices simple and clear: one adult price, and optionally a child price and a price for a private group.

    The child price should not be higher than the adult price.
    A private group usually costs at least as much as the minimum number of guests at the adult price.
    –
    The maximum should be bigger than the minimum.

    5 Season & Plan B

    When is this experience available? And what do you offer instead if it rains or it's out of season — at the same price, so the booking doesn't need to change.
